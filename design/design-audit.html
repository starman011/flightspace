ObjectTracer · Design Audit

One rounded language,
one sheet of glass.

The app already has the right instincts — dark cockpit canvas, lime readout accent, monospace data. What breaks the spell is inconsistency: six different corner radii, five blur strengths, ad-hoc padding. This audit collapses all of it into one system, so the three principles stop being aspirations and become defaults.

The system

4 radii · 1 glass · 1 accent

Everything downstream is derived from this. The signature move: every glass surface carries a bright top-edge rim (a 16%-white border-top) — the same glossy top the new app-icon logo has. It reads as light catching the lip of real glass, and it ties the entire UI back to the brand mark.

Desktop

the globe, uninterrupted

Controls float as glass islands over the globe and never box it in. The detail card leads with the callsign and route — the two things a viewer actually wants — then drops to a clean 2×2 of readouts. Every island shares the exact radius, blur, and top-rim, so they read as one family hovering at the same altitude.

Mobile

the sheet that follows your thumb

Same family, restacked for one hand. Pages pill hugs the top-left so it never collides with the account avatar. The detail card becomes a bottom sheet with a real grab handle — it tracks the finger 1:1 and a single flick expands it fully (the old sheet needed several drags). Everything keeps the 22px top corners and the glass rim.

What actually changes

the punch list

Today

  • Radii scattered across 2, 4, 10, 12, 14, 16, 18, 20px — nothing feels like one product
  • Blur ranges 12–32px per component; glass panels sit at different "depths"
  • Detail card leads with logo + type code, not the callsign & route
  • Mobile sheet has no finger-tracking; needs several drags to open
  • Emoji still in a few spots (ISS crew, status glyphs)

Proposed

  • Exactly 4 radii with fixed roles — pill / 22 / 14 / 10
  • One glass recipe + a gloss top-rim that ties every surface to the logo
  • Detail card reorders to callsign → route → 2×2 readouts → one CTA
  • Bottom sheet tracks the thumb; one flick = full (already shipped)
  • Replace remaining emoji with the SVG icon set for a single visual voice

## 3. Description (Product Hunt body)

> ObjectTracer is a real-time 3D globe that tracks everything moving above you —
> live flights (ADS-B), ships (AIS), the ISS with its 4K NASA stream and crew,
> satellites, rocket launches, near-Earth asteroids, and even deep-space galaxies.
>
> Most trackers show one thing on a flat 2D map. ObjectTracer puts it all on one
> interactive globe you can spin from street level out to the cosmic web. Click any
> aircraft for live altitude/speed/route, watch the ISS pass overhead, or count down
> to the next SpaceX launch.
>
> Free, no signup, works in the browser. Built for plane spotters, space nerds, and
> anyone who's ever looked up and wondered "what is that?"

## Reality check (keep expectations honest)

- Launch spike traffic is **temporary** (days). Its real value is the **backlinks +
  crawl-budget boost** that compound over months.
- Long-tail pages (specific callsigns, small airports, asteroid names) rank first —
  weeks, not months.
- Head terms ("ISS tracker", "flight tracker") need sustained backlinks + age —
  6–12 months. Don't measure success by those early.
